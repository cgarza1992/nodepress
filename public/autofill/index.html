One saved profile fills the forms for you, with a review step every time. It runs only when you click it, keeps everything on your own computer, and never submits anything for you.

Setup

Chromium browsers (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc) load an extension folder directly. It takes a few minutes, and you only do most of this once.

1
about 2 minutes

Install the extension

You are loading a folder from your own computer. This is normal and safe.

  1. Unzip the file you just downloaded. You get a folder with a manifest.json inside it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (paste that into the address bar).
  3. Turn on Developer mode, the toggle in the top right.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Pin it to your toolbar: click the puzzle-piece icon, then the pin next to it.
Works inChrome, Edge, Brave, Arc, and other Chromium browsers. Safari and Firefox are not supported.
2
about 4 minutes

Add your profile

This is what the extension types into forms. The more complete it is, the more it can fill for you.

  1. Right-click the extension icon and choose Options (or open it from the popup).
  2. Fill in your name and contact details, then your work history.
  3. Save. That is your reusable profile, and you only do this once.
PrivateYour profile is stored only in this browser, on this computer. It is not uploaded anywhere.
3
optional, recommended

Upload your documents

So the extension can attach them on forms that ask for a file.

  • Résumé: PDF, DOC, or DOCX.
  • Cover letter: use a .docx if you want the tailor-per-job feature below.
Cover-letter tipWrite [Company] and [Role] in your letter where they should change per job. In Options, Cover letter replacements, give constants like [Portfolio] a value, and leave per-application ones like [Hiring Manager] blank to fill them each time.
4
optional

Turn on the power-ups, or don’t

Both are off until you set them up, and both keep your data yours.

  • Local AI drafting: point it at a model running on your own machine to draft open-ended answers and cover letters. Nothing leaves your computer.
  • Notion logging: connect your own Notion to record every application you send. Bring your own key.
5
the main event

Fill your first form

Everything above was one-time setup. This is what you do on every application.

  1. Open a job-application page.
  2. Click the extension, or press Ctrl Shift Y (⌘ Shift Y on Mac).
  3. A review panel opens with three lists: Will fill, Needs your check, and Skipped.
  4. Look it over, fix anything, then approve. It types your answers in, and stops there.
You stay in controlIt never clicks Submit. You read the filled form and send the application yourself.
6
if you saved a .docx cover letter

Tailor your cover letter

Turn one template into a letter written for this exact posting.

  1. In the panel, choose Fill my cover letter.
  2. It swaps [Company], [Role], and your placeholders for this job.
  3. Preview it, then Attach to this application or Download a copy.
GuardrailIt will not attach a letter that still has an unfilled [placeholder] in it, so a half-finished template cannot go out by mistake.
Peace of mind

What it will never do

  • Submit anything on its own.It fills the form and stops. You always press send.
  • Fill demographic questions on its own.EEO and voluntary self-identification are skipped by default. If you want them answered, you opt in from Settings and give your own values.
  • Send your data away.Your profile and documents live in your browser. No servers, no accounts.

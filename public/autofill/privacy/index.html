Privacy Policy

Job Application Autofill (Chrome extension) · Last updated August 10, 2026

The short version: everything you enter stays on your computer, in your browser. The extension sends none of your data to the developer. There is no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and nothing is ever sold.

Job Application Autofill is an accessibility tool that types your saved details into job-application forms. You set up a profile once; on an application you click the icon and it fills what it can, flags what needs your check, and skips what it is unsure about. You review everything before anything is entered, and it never clicks Submit.

What is stored, and where

Your profile, any resume or cover-letter files you add, and your settings are stored locally in your browser using chrome.storage.local, on your own device. They are not uploaded to the developer or to any server operated by the developer.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The extension contains no analytics or telemetry, and the developer receives no information about you or how you use it.

Optional integrations you turn on yourself

  • Local AI (Ollama): off by default. If you enable it, resume or answer text is sent only to a model running on your own machine (localhost). It does not leave your computer.
  • Notion logging: off by default. If you add your own Notion token, your application log syncs directly from your browser to your own Notion workspace. The developer is not involved and never sees your token or your data.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • storage — save your profile, documents, and settings locally.
  • activeTab + scripting — read and fill the form only on the tab you explicitly run it on, when you click. There is no background or all-sites access.
  • host access to localhost:11434 — talk to your own local Ollama, only if you enable AI features.
  • host access to api.notion.com — optional application logging to your own Notion.

Sensitive fields

The extension does not fill or submit demographic, EEO, or voluntary self-identification fields unless you explicitly opt in and provide your own answers.

Data sharing and selling

The developer does not collect, share, transfer, or sell any user data.

Changes
